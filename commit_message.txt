Fix title extraction for dynamic guidance with overall recommendation feature
Enhanced DynamicGuidanceParser to handle AI responses that don't follow the expected [TITLE] format. The AI was returning
the title as the first section name instead of in a dedicated [TITLE] section, causing "Parenting Situation" fallback to
display.
Changes:
- Added fallback logic to extract first bracketed section as title when no [TITLE] section exists
- Updated section extraction to skip first section if it was used as the title
- Added comprehensive logging to capture full AI response content for debugging
- Maintains backward compatibility with proper [TITLE] formatted responses
This ensures the overall recommendation feature displays correct guidance titles like "Talking to Your Child About
Staying for After-School Activities" instead of generic fallbacks.
