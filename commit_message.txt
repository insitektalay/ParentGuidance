Add coping strategies toggle to Guidance Structure settings

Implemented a new toggle in the Guidance Structure section that allows users to control whether existing coping 
strategies are included in AI guidance generation. When enabled, the app fetches previously identified coping strategies 
from the database and includes them as context in all prompt templates.

Added the toggle UI with localization, updated all 8 prompt templates to include the new 
coping_strategies_home_consequences variable, and integrated data fetching throughout the service layer. The feature 
respects user preferences and only includes coping strategies data when the toggle is enabled, providing more contextual 
and personalized parenting guidance.
